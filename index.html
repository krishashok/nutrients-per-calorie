The method

Ten nutrients, one hundred calories

Each nutrient contributes the percentage of your daily requirement that 100 kcal of the food delivers, capped so one freak vitamin cannot carry a food on its own. The ten percentages are added up.

The catch

Unless you are a cow, mind the dot size

The score is per 100 kcal, and 100 kcal of palak is nearly half a kilo of it. Curry leaves outscore chicken breast, but nobody eats them by the bowl. A high score counts only in quantities you can actually eat, which is why the dot size shows a practical serving.

Reading the axes

High and right is good

Up means more nutrition per calorie. Right means more protein per calorie. Staples sit bottom left because their job is calories. Fish, liver and leafy greens sit top right because they do everything else.

The intelligent choice is boring
Keep your rice. Make it share the plate.

A dal, a sabzi, some curd and two eggs do the nutritional work the rice does not. If you eat meat, liver and small fish give you more per rupee than anything else on this chart.
